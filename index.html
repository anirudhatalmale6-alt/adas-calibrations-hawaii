What We Do

Comprehensive ADAS Services

From initial diagnostic scanning to final post-calibration verification, we handle every step of the ADAS calibration process.

01

ADAS Calibration

Static and dynamic calibration of forward-facing cameras, radar sensors, and lidar systems to OEM specifications.

02

Sensor Calibration

Precision alignment and calibration of all vehicle safety sensors including blind-spot monitors, parking sensors, and cross-traffic alerts.

03

Diagnostic Scanning

Comprehensive pre- and post-repair diagnostic scans to identify all ADAS fault codes and verify system functionality.

04

Module Programming

Factory-level module programming and software updates using OEM-licensed tools for accurate system configuration.

05

Wheel Alignment Verification

Thrust angle and alignment verification to ensure ADAS sensors reference accurate vehicle geometry for proper calibration.

06

Documentation & Reports

Complete calibration documentation including pre/post scan reports, calibration certificates, and code clearing records.

Our Story

Hawaii's Calibration Experts

ADAS Calibrations of Hawaii was built from the ground up with one mission: to provide Honolulu and the Hawaiian Islands with a world-class ADAS calibration facility that meets the highest OEM standards.

Before a single vehicle came through the door, our team worked through the specific physical requirements a proper calibration environment demands -- grinding and planing the shop floor to certified levelness, installing controlled lighting, and configuring every inch of the 3,000 sq. ft. space to meet OEM calibration standards.

"We didn't come into a space that was already set up. We did it from day one and went through that process to really understand why every single requirement matters."
-- Gerard AhQuin, Manager

As a licensed Car ADAS Solutions facility, we bring national-level calibration expertise to Hawaii's automotive industry, ensuring that every vehicle leaving our shop has its safety systems calibrated to factory precision.
